discovery/report system, piracy and bounties, tournaments, the full-world-map
expansion, and the retirement/victory condition. Gates **Phase 3**. Schemas

## 4. Systems / Mechanics

### 4.1 Fame (new stat)

- `player.fame` integer ≥ 0, Phase-3 save field. Sources: quest completion
  (`Quest.rewards.fame`), discovery reports, tournament wins, royal missions.
- Fame is permanent (no decay) and is the currency of the title ladder (§4.3).

### 4.2 Guild quests

- **Offers:** each port guild lists 3 quests; the offer list refreshes every
  7 in-game days and after any completion. Generated by `quest_gen.gd`,
  seeded by (port id, day // 7).
- **Types** (`Quest.type`, schema `10-*` §4.8):
  - `delivery`: pick up `quantity` of `good_id` (given free as cargo, must
    fit) and deliver to `dest_port_id` within `expiry_days`. Reward ≈
    `2 × base_price × quantity` gold, +5 rep with the port's nation, +10 fame.
    Fail (expired or cargo sold/jettisoned): −5 rep, no fame.
  - `bounty`: a named pirate fleet spawns at a fixed zone; sink/capture it
    within `expiry_days` (uses `60-*`). Reward 5,000–20,000g by target tier,
    +15 fame.
  - `exploration`: grants the `region_hint` of one undiscovered `Discovery`;
    reporting that discovery completes the quest in addition to normal
    report rewards.
  - `royal` quests exist only via the palace (§4.3).
- **Tracking:** max 3 active quests; the quests tab of the character panel
  lists objective, destination marker (shows the dest port even if unknown —
  quest reveals it), and days remaining.
- Hand-authored `data/quests.json` entries act as **templates**: the
  generator fills `requirements`/`rewards` per the bands above; purely static
  story quests are out of the design (no scripted storylines, `00-*` §6).

### 4.3 Royal missions & nobility titles

- **Palace access:** capital ports only (`10-*` §7.4). A nation offers royal
  missions only if the player's reputation with it ≥ 30.
- **Mission kinds:** royal delivery (as `delivery` but reward ×3 and +10
  rep), naval patrol (defeat 2 navy-marked hostile fleets of a rival nation),
  charting (report 2 discoveries). One royal mission at a time per nation.
- **Title ladder (per nation):** Knight → Baron → Viscount → Count → Duke.

| Rank | Fame required | Royal missions required | Grant |
|---|---|---|---|
| Knight | 100 | 1 | +5% sell prices at that nation's ports |
| Baron | 300 | 2 | +5% more (total 10%) |
| Viscount | 700 | 3 | +1 max active quest slot |
| Count | 1500 | 5 | palace bays: repair cost ×0.8 at that nation |
| Duke | 3000 | 8 | retirement eligible (§4.7) |

- Title grant is a palace ceremony dialog; per-nation independent ladders.

### 4.4 Discovery & report

- A `Discovery` (`10-*` §4.7) is found by moving within Chebyshev distance 3
  of its `position` and choosing **Explore** (world-map action when the HUD
  shows the `world.something_near` hint). First discovery only counts;
  repeats are ignored.
- **Report:** at any guild; reward = `report_reward_gold` + `fame`; removed
  from the unreported list.
- `region_hint` strings are sold at taverns for 100g (rumor button, extends
  `30-*` tavern with a third tab `Rumors`); exploration quests give hints
  free.

### 4.5 Piracy & bounty

- **Piracy acts:** attacking a navy marker (`60-*` §4.1), sinking/capturing
  national ships: rep −20 with that nation per act; rep with its rivals
  (defined per `70-*` task as top-2 by port-count distance — task-owned
  table) +2.
- **Bounty:** rep ≤ −50 → that nation issues a bounty: navy hunter fleets
  spawn per `60-*` §4.1; its ports are closed to the player (facility rows
  show `port.closed_bounty`).
- **Clearing:** pay the bounty at that nation's palace (cost =
  `500g × |rep|`), or surrender the flagship to a hunter (`60-*` §4.7) — both
  reset rep to −10.

### 4.6 Tournaments

- Monthly: on day 1 of each 30-day cycle, one capital (rotating in the
  §7.4 order) hosts a tournament; announced via tavern rumor and the guild.
- Entry: at that port's palace during cycle days 1–5, fee 1,000g.
- Format: 3 successive `60-*` battles vs. AI fleets of escalating tier
  (brigantine → carrack → galleon class), durability carries between rounds,
  no repairs between rounds. Win all 3: prize 10,000g + 100 fame; loss at any
  round: consolation 500g, ships repaired to 50% free.

### 4.7 Victory / retirement

- **Condition:** Duke rank with **any** nation (fame ≥ 3000 + 8 royal
  missions).
- Familiar flow: palace offers **Retire** → ending screen (score summary:
  days sailed, gold, fame, titles, discoveries count, battles won) → the save
  is marked `retired` (load shows summary, not playable).
- There is no fail-ending besides the death game-overs; retirement is the
  win state. Continuing to play after declining retirement is allowed.

### 4.8 Full world map (content scope)

- New file `data/maps/p3_world.txt` with its own legend entry set; ~100 ports
  worldwide (Americas, Africa, India, East Asia) as data-only content per
  `90-*` §10. Exact port list, grid size, and coordinates are **task-owned**
  by `IMPLEMENT-Phase3.md` (they must extend, not alter, the Phase-1 grid's
  semantics; a separate grid size is allowed since map loading is
  legend-driven).
- Phase-1 map remains playable; new-game nation packages unchanged (trade
  routes to the new world are the Phase-3 economy driver).

## 6. Edge Cases & Fail States

- **Delivery goods sold mid-route** → quest fails immediately (checked at
  every cargo mutation).
- **Expired bounty quest while in battle with the target** → quest completes
  if the battle wins; the clock checks at battle end.
- **Discovery at a tile unreachable in the Phase-1 map** → generator/config
  error; validator (extended in Phase 3 tasks) checks positions ⊆ the active
  world map.
- **Rep ≤ −50 with own nation** → allowed; own ports close, bounty via own
  palace (recovery always possible ⇒ no softlock).
- **Tournament while bounty-hunted by host nation** → palace closed; no
  entry (cycle passes).
- **Retired save** → load button shows summary screen only; delete via
  normal slot overwrite.
- **Acting pirate with 0 cannons (ram-only fleets)** → legal; piracy flags
  on aggression regardless of means.
